---
name: Public readers and private administration
description: The user's standing product rule for public browsing, selective login prompts, and separation of admin content.
---

Keep backend administrator content and access clearly separate from the frontend content that readers view. Users must not have to log in merely to view the app; request login only for particular services that need it.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a clear separation and delegated the choice of which services require login.

**How to apply:** Preserve a public-first reader experience when adding screens or services. Do not put internal administrator tools into ordinary reader navigation or introduce a site-wide mandatory login gate.
